Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026.

Aevum

Aevum does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The app makes no network connections. Everything it draws is computed on your device.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics or tracking, and contains no third-party code.
  • Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

If you contact us at support@ovumcode.com, we use your email address and whatever you choose to send only to reply to you, and we do not share it.
